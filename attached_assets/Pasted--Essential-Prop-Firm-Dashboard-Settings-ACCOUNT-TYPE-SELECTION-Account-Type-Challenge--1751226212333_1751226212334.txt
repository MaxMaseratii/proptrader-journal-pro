# Essential Prop Firm Dashboard Settings

## ACCOUNT TYPE SELECTION
- **Account Type**: [Challenge] | [Funded Account] | [Live Account]

---

## CHALLENGE/EVALUATION SETTINGS

### Basic Info
- **Firm Name**: Text field
- **Account Size**: Dropdown ($10K, $25K, $50K, $100K, $150K, $200K, $250K, $300K)
- **Account Cost**: Currency field
- **Number of Phases**: [1] | [2]

### Targets & Time
- **Phase 1 Target**: Percentage OR Fixed Amount
- **Phase 2 Target**: Percentage OR Fixed Amount *(if 2-phase)*
- **Minimum Trading Days**: Number field
- **Time Limit**: Days (0 = unlimited)

### Drawdown Rules *(Critical)*
- **Drawdown Type**: 
  - Daily Drawdown (realized losses only)
  - Unrealized Drawdown (includes open positions)
  - End of Day Trailing
  - Account Balance Based
- **Daily Loss Limit**: Percentage OR Fixed Amount
- **Max Total Loss**: Percentage OR Fixed Amount
- **Trailing Threshold**: Fixed Amount *(if applicable)*

### Key Restrictions
- **Consistency Rule**: [YES] | [NO]
- **Consistency %**: Percentage *(if YES)*
- **Copy Trading**: [Allowed] | [Not Allowed]
- **News Trading**: [Allowed] | [Not Allowed]

---

## FUNDED ACCOUNT SETTINGS

### Payout Requirements *(Essential)*
- **Days Required for Payout**: Number field
- **Winning Day Minimum**: Currency amount *(if applicable)*
- **Minimum Payout Amount**: Currency field
- **Payout Frequency**: [Daily] | [Weekly] | [Bi-weekly] | [Monthly] | [On-demand]

### Payout Rules
- **Maximum Payout %**: Percentage of balance
- **Account Buffer Required**: [YES] | [NO]
- **Buffer Amount**: Currency *(if YES)*
- **Profit Split**: Percentage to trader

### Funded Drawdown *(Critical)*
- **Drawdown Type**: 
  - Daily Drawdown (realized losses only)
  - Unrealized Drawdown (includes open positions) 
  - End of Day Trailing
  - No Drawdown Limits
- **Daily Loss Limit**: Percentage OR Fixed Amount
- **Max Loss Limit**: Fixed Amount (often $0)

### Key Rules
- **Consistency Rule**: [YES] | [NO]
- **Copy Trading**: [Allowed] | [Not Allowed]
- **Enhanced Payouts Available**: [YES] | [NO] *(e.g., daily payouts, 100% withdrawals)*

---

## LIVE ACCOUNT SETTINGS *(If Available)*

### Live Transition
- **Live Account Available**: [YES] | [NO]
- **Transition Trigger**: Text field *(e.g., "After 5 payouts")*
- **Activation Cost**: Currency field

### Live Rules
- **Drawdown Type**: Same options as funded
- **Payout Benefits**: Text field *(e.g., "Daily payouts, 100% withdrawals")*
- **Starting Balance**: [Funded Balance] | [Capped Amount]

---

## RISK MANAGEMENT *(Universal)*

### Risk Per Trade
- **Risk Method**: 
  - Percentage of Account Balance
  - Fixed Dollar Amount
  - Percentage of Daily Limit
- **Risk Amount**: Percentage OR Currency
- **Max Daily Risk**: Percentage OR Currency

### Risk Controls
- **Stop Loss Required**: [YES] | [NO]
- **Max Risk/Reward Ratio**: Ratio field *(e.g., 1:1, 1:2)*

## DASHBOARD DISPLAY

### Real-Time Tracking
- **Current Balance**: Auto-updated
- **Profit/Loss Today**: Auto-calculated
- **Drawdown Status**: Auto-monitored with alerts
- **Days Traded**: Auto-count
- **Target Progress**: Visual progress bar
- **Payout Eligibility**: [Eligible] | [Not Eligible] | [X days remaining]

### Risk Monitor
- **Risk Used Today**: Percentage of daily limit
- **Current Position Risk**: Live calculation
- **Drawdown Warning**: Alert system at 80% of limit

### Quick Actions
- **New Trade Entry**: Risk calculator
- **Close All Positions**: Emergency button
- **Request Payout**: If eligible
- **View Progress**: Detailed stats
