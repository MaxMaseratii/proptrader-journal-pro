⚠️ Common Restrictions
What's Usually NOT Allowed:

News Trading (around major announcements)
Scalping (sub-minute trades) - Some firms
Grid Trading or Martingale strategies
Copy Trading or EA trading (varies)
Weekend Gap Trading
Illiquid instruments

Risk Limitations:

Maximum position size per trade
Daily loss limits (typically 3-5%)
Maximum loss limits (typically 6-10%)
Correlation limits (similar positions)
Overnight holding restrictions (some firms)

💡 Pro Tips for Asset Selection:
Best Assets for Beginners:

ES (S&P 500 futures) - Consistent liquidity
EUR/USD - Tight spreads, predictable
Large cap stocks - Less volatile

High Profit Potential (Higher Risk):

NQ (Nasdaq futures) - High volatility
GBP/USD - Major price swings
CL (Crude Oil) - Energy sector volatility

Factors to Consider:

Trading session (US, European, Asian)
Spread costs and commissions
Volatility levels
Your trading style (scalping, swing, day trading)
Capital requirements

Most prop firms focus on liquid, major instruments rather than exotic or low-volume assets. The key is choosing instruments that match your trading style and the firm's rules!
