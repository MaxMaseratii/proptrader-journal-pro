# PropJournal Pro - Journal Capabilities & Focus

## 🎯 Core Philosophy

PropJournal Pro's trading journal is built on the principle that **consistent self-reflection drives trading success**. Unlike simple trade logs, this journal focuses on the psychological and decision-making aspects of trading that separate profitable traders from those who struggle.

## 📝 Journal Structure & Features

### Three-Pillar Reflection System

Every journal entry follows a structured approach designed by trading psychologists:

**1. What Went Wrong?**
- Identifies mistakes, emotional decisions, and rule violations
- Encourages honest self-assessment without judgment
- Tracks patterns of recurring issues
- Helps traders recognize their weak points

**2. What Went Right?**
- Celebrates successful strategies and disciplined decisions
- Reinforces positive trading behaviors
- Builds confidence through documented wins
- Creates a reference library of personal best practices

**3. Tomorrow's Improvement Plan**
- Sets specific, actionable goals for the next trading session
- Creates accountability through written commitments
- Focuses on continuous improvement rather than perfection
- Transforms insights into concrete action steps

### Account-Specific Journaling

- **Multi-Account Support**: Separate journal entries for each trading account
- **Account Context**: Each entry is tied to specific account performance and rules
- **Prop Firm Alignment**: Journal prompts align with prop firm evaluation criteria
- **Challenge Tracking**: Special focus on challenge-specific goals and restrictions

### Date-Based Organization

- **Daily Entries**: One comprehensive entry per trading day
- **Calendar Integration**: Easy navigation through trading history
- **Historical Review**: Access to all previous entries for pattern recognition
- **Timeline Visualization**: See trading evolution over time

## 🎯 Primary Focus Areas

### 1. Risk Management Psychology
The journal emphasizes psychological aspects of risk management:
- Emotional state during high-risk decisions
- Adherence to position sizing rules
- Daily loss limit respect and decision-making under pressure
- Exit strategy execution and discipline

### 2. Rule Adherence Tracking
Specifically designed for prop traders who must follow strict rules:
- Daily drawdown limit violations and near-misses
- Consistency rule compliance
- Trading hour restrictions and discipline
- Forbidden instrument avoidance

### 3. Performance Pattern Recognition
Helps traders identify recurring patterns:
- Time-of-day performance variations
- Market condition responses
- Emotional trading triggers
- Successful vs. unsuccessful setups

### 4. Continuous Improvement Mindset
Transforms daily trading into learning opportunities:
- Progressive skill development tracking
- Goal setting and achievement monitoring
- Mistake prevention through documented learning
- Confidence building through success recognition
